Questions people ask on the porch

How long does pre-approval take?

Often the same day once your documents are in. Derek reviews every file himself before it goes out.

How much do I really need for a down payment?

Less than most people think. Some loans start at 3% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA at zero for eligible buyers.

I'm moving to Charleston from out of state. Can we start now?

Yes. Get approved before you fly in, so you can make an offer the same weekend you find the house.

What about flood insurance?

It matters here. Derek helps you check the flood zone and estimate insurance before you write an offer.

I'm self-employed. Is that a problem?

No. There are loan options built for business owners and entrepreneurs, and Derek works with them all the time.
