Meet Derek

Derek Goulette runs Fairway Home Mortgage's Daniel Island branch. His favorite part of the job is the conversation: sitting down with buyers and Realtors, figuring out what the right move actually is, and explaining it so plainly it's obvious.

That's why he answers his own phone, why he keeps every client and agent in the loop, and why so much of his business comes from people who've already worked with him.
