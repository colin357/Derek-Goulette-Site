Beyond the payment

What the
calculator misses.

The real monthly cost of a Charleston home has a few more lines. Derek builds all of them into your plan.

01

Property taxes

South Carolina taxes primary homes at a lower assessment ratio than second homes and rentals, which can change your payment a lot.

02

Flood & wind

Depending on the flood zone and how close you are to the coast, insurance can be a meaningful share of the payment.

03

HOA dues

Many Lowcountry neighborhoods have HOAs. Some include amenities and exterior maintenance; some don't.

04

Mortgage insurance

Under 20% down on conventional, or any FHA loan, adds mortgage insurance. VA loans have none.
