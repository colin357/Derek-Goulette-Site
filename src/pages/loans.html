FHA
loans.

Backed by the Federal Housing Administration, FHA loans are more flexible on credit history and debt-to-income, which makes them a strong option if you're building or rebuilding credit.

  • Down payment From 3.5%
  • Mortgage insurance Upfront and annual premium
  • Credit More flexible guidelines
  • Best for First-time and credit-building buyers

03

VA
loans.

You earned this benefit. VA loans let eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses buy with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. Stationed at Joint Base Charleston or heading here on orders? Start before you arrive.

  • Down payment As little as 0%
  • Monthly mortgage insurance None
  • VA funding fee May apply; some are exempt
  • Best for Veterans and military families

Questions

Straight
answers.

Don't see yours? Ask Derek directly. He'd rather answer a question than have you guess.

How long does pre-approval take?

Often the same day once your documents are in. Derek reviews files personally before a pre-approval goes out, so it holds up when it counts.

How much do I really need for a down payment?

Usually less than people think. Conventional loans can start at 3% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA at zero for eligible buyers. Down-payment assistance may also be available.

Does flood insurance affect what I can afford?

In the Lowcountry, often yes. Derek helps you check the flood zone and estimate insurance before you write an offer, so your payment doesn't surprise you at closing.

I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Yes. There are programs built for business owners and entrepreneurs. Bring your last two years of returns and Derek will map out your options.

Should I wait for rates to drop?

It depends on your situation. Derek will run both scenarios with you, including what a future refinance could look like, so you can decide with real numbers.
