Derek Goulette leads Fairway Home Mortgage's Daniel Island branch, helping families across the Charleston area buy, build and refinance homes, from a first condo in West Ashley to a waterfront home on the islands.

What he loves most about the work is the conversation. Sitting down with a first-time buyer who thinks owning is out of reach and showing them it isn't. Getting a call from a Realtor on a Friday night and solving the problem before Monday. That's also why he started filming short videos: most mortgage questions have a simple answer, and people deserve to hear it plainly.

His clients are young professionals, entrepreneurs, growing families, military households and investors. His specialties include first-time buyers, luxury and jumbo loans, investment properties, FHA and VA loans, relocation and refinancing.

Today Derek is also building the branch, mentoring loan officers and investing in the marketing and systems that let his team spend more time with clients and less time on paperwork.
